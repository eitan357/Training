chart.js loaded from external CDN  Info

The app fetches chart.js@4 from cdn.jsdelivr.net. This is logged by the performance test as an unknown third-party origin (soft check, does not fail the test).

Implications: users with network restrictions or CDN downtime won't see charts. CDNs log IP addresses. Consider self-hosting or bundling chart.js. Not blocking.
